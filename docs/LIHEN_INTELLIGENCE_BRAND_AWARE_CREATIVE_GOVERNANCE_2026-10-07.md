# LIHEN Intelligence — Brand-aware Creative Governance

## Objective

Make LIHEN Intelligence explicitly aware of LIHEN brand identity before
creative, visual and editorial recommendations are prepared.

This does not grant execution authority.

## Canonical brand context

Intelligence now carries canonical guidance for:

- LIHEN.CO | Beauty Care • Style;
- official brand signature;
- official-logo preservation;
- pastel blush / lavender / cream / soft-lime palette;
- gold/copper details;
- Beauty Care visual direction;
- Style visual direction;
- GENERATED != OFFICIAL;
- RECOMMENDATION != EXECUTION;
- MESSAGE GENERATION != SENDING.

## Creative QA

The Creative Intelligence pipeline applies brand constraints before invoking
an image-generation provider.

A deterministic audit contract supports:

- brand match;
- logo integrity;
- copy;
- channel fit;
- execution state.

Logo observations can classify:

- official transparent logo;
- accidental opaque white background;
- distorted logo;
- unverified logo;
- absent logo.

An accidental white background produces a warning and recommends the official
transparent logo or reversible background removal without modifying the symbol,
wordmark, color or proportions.

A request to redesign or reinterpret the official logo fails closed.

## Important limitation

The core does not pretend to see pixels when no visual-observation provider has
supplied an observation.

When no visual observation exists, logo integrity remains NOT_ASSESSED and
human visual review is required.

This is intentional fail-closed behavior.
